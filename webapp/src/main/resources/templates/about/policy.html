Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data elephantchess.io collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and how you can view or delete it.

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

{{policy_toc}}

Who we are

elephantchess.io is a free website and Android app to play Chinese Chess (Xiangqi) and Korean Chess (Janggi) online, against bots, or through puzzles. The website and app are operated by Encelade (a company registered in Belgium). The Android app is a thin wrapper around this same website, so this policy covers both.

The service is free to use and does not run ads inside the app. If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at info@elephantchess.io or through our contact form.

What data we collect

You can use the webapp as a guest, without creating an account. A guest session is temporary and tied to your device through a cookie. If you sign up, we additionally store the data you provide and the activity linked to your account.

  • Account data (registered users only): your email address, username and chosen country. The email is used to log in, recover your password and send you account-related messages.
  • Session and technical data: for each session we record the IP address, approximate location (country, region, city) derived from it, operating system and browser (user agent), and the times the session was created and last active. This helps us understand where our users come from and when they were last online.
  • Page views: we record the pages you visit on the site to understand how it is used.
  • Game and puzzle activity: the games you play, your moves, ratings and the puzzles you solve.
  • Chat messages: messages you send in a game.

We do not ask for or store any special-category data (such as health, religion or political opinions), and we do not knowingly collect more than what is described above.

Cookies

Cookies are small pieces of information stored in your browser that a website can read and write when you visit. We use necessary cookies (1) to keep you logged in across visits and (2) to remember your preferences, such as piece style and move format. These cookies are sent to the server on each request but that information is not stored. They expire after 400 days (the maximum allowed) and are listed under the "Necessary" category in the cookie consent banner (or behind the cookies icon once you have accepted or refused). Cookies set by elephantchess.io cannot be used to track you outside of our website.

With your consent, we also load third-party tools that set their own cookies: Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager (to understand traffic) and Google Ads (to bring in visitors). Embedded YouTube videos set cookies as well, which means a video you watch here may also appear in your YouTube history. You can refuse these in the consent banner at the bottom of the page; refusing does not affect your ability to play.

We also use Google reCAPTCHA on some forms (for example sign-up) to protect the service against abuse; this is provided by Google and subject to Google's privacy policy.

Third-party services

We do not sell your data and we do not share your email or your activity with advertisers. We do rely on a small number of external services to run the site:

  • Google (Analytics, Tag Manager, Ads, reCAPTCHA and YouTube embeds) — loaded only after you accept the relevant cookies.
  • emaillistverify.com — used to validate email addresses at sign-up so we avoid bounced emails, which could harm our ability to send legitimate messages.
  • Ko-fi and PayPal — only if you choose to support the project financially; payment is handled on their own platforms.
  • Discord — if you choose to join our community server, that happens on Discord's platform.

How long we keep your data

Inactive guest accounts (no games or puzzles played for {{guest_retention_days}} days) are archived and their personal data is removed.

Account data is kept for as long as your account exists. Necessary cookies expire after 400 days. You can delete your data at any time as described below.

Viewing and deleting your data

Registered users can view and manage their data in their account settings:

  • See your latest sessions (and the stored IP/location data) and delete them.
  • See your latest page views and archive them immediately with the "archive my views now" button, which detaches them from your account straight away instead of waiting for the automatic archival.

As a guest, you can obtain a fresh, unlinked session at any time by deleting the elephantchess.io cookies in your browser. To delete your account entirely, or to request a copy or erasure of your data, contact us at info@elephantchess.io or via the contact form.

Public data

Some information is public by design. Chat messages are public and not encrypted: they can be seen by anyone with the game link and reviewed by admins and moderators. Games played on the webapp are public, and the game data is available under the GPL-3.0 license. The published datasets are anonymized. Non-anonymized game data (showing usernames, but without email address, IP or any other personal data) may be shared with third parties for research or technical purposes.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union, the GDPR gives you the right to access, correct, export or erase your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise most of these rights directly in your account settings, or by contacting us at info@elephantchess.io. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

elephantchess.io is a general-audience board-game service and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes may also be announced on the site.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your personal data, reach out at info@elephantchess.io or through our contact form.
