Delete Account

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Deleting your account permanently removes your personal data: your email address, username, password and the sessions we store about you (including their IP addresses). You will be logged out immediately and will not be able to log back in.

Your games, bot games, puzzle results and ratings are not deleted: they are detached from your identity and transferred to a new anonymized account with a different id, so they can no longer be linked back to you. This keeps the games you played with other people intact.

If you only want to remove your stored sessions or page views instead, you can do that from your account settings.
