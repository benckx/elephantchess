Delete Account

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

In accordance with GDPR and other privacy regulations as well as Google Play Store policies, you are allowed to delete all personal data.

Deleting your account permanently removes your personal data: your email address, username, password, page views and user sessions (IP addresses and derived location). You will be logged out immediately and will not be able to log back in with this account. This information may still be present in our technical backups for up to 14 days.

Your games, bot games, puzzle results and ratings are not deleted: they are detached from your identity and transferred to a new anonymized account with a different id, so they can no longer be linked back to you. This is done to keep history and rating consistency for other players.

Your username will become available again to other users who sign up.

If you only want to remove your stored sessions or page views instead, or disable the email notifications, you can do that from your account settings.

We would be sorry to see you go, but you can always come back and play with us.

This option is only applicable if you are authenticated.
