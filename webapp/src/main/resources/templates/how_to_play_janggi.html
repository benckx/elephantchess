How to Play Janggi

Janggi is Korean chess. Like Xiangqi, it is played on a 9 by 10 board, except that the board doesn't have a river. It uses Blue and Red pieces where Xiangqi uses Red and Black (so the Red ones are on the other side). Blue moves first. Click or drag pieces on the interactive boards below to explore their moves; use each reset link to restore its example.

The boards use westernized pieces by default. Change piece style in the settings if you prefer traditional Janggi characters.

Pieces and moves

General (초 or 楚 / 한 or 漢)

The General moves one point orthogonally or along a marked palace diagonal. It must stay inside its 3 by 3 palace.

It is represented by the letter K in FEN.

reset / test board

Guard (사 or 士)

The Guard moves like the General: one point orthogonally or along a marked palace diagonal. It cannot leave its palace.

It is represented by the letter A in FEN.

reset / test board

Chariot (차 or 車)

The Chariot moves any distance orthogonally. It can also follow the marked diagonal lines through either palace. Pieces block its path.

It is similar to the Rook in western chess or the Chariot in Xiangqi.

It is represented by the letter R in FEN.

reset / test board

Cannon (포 or 包)

The Cannon must jump over exactly one piece to move or capture. It cannot jump over another Cannon or capture one.

It is represented by the letter C in FEN.

reset / test board

Horse (마 or 馬)

The Horse moves one point orthogonally, then one point diagonally outward. A piece on the first point blocks the move. It is similar to the Horse in Xiangqi.

It is represented by the letter N in FEN.

reset / test board

Elephant (상 or 象)

The Elephant moves one point orthogonally, then two points diagonally in the same direction. Pieces on either diagonal step block the move.

It is represented by the letter B in FEN.

reset / test board

Soldier (졸 or 卒 / 병 or 兵)

The Soldier moves one point forward or sideways, but never backward. Inside the opponent's palace, it can also move forward along marked diagonal lines.

It is represented by the letter P in FEN.

Setup

Each player starts with one General, two Guards, two Elephants, two Horses, two Chariots, two Cannons, and five Soldiers. The General and Guards start in the palace. Each player chooses their starting arrangement independently. The following setups show the back rank from left to right in FEN order, with R = Chariot, N = Horse, B = Elephant, A = Guard, and 1 = an empty point:

  • Inner Elephant: rnba1abnr (the default).
  • Outer Elephant: rbna1anbr.
  • Right Elephant: rnba1anbr.
  • Left Elephant: rbna1abnr.
  • Central Chariot: bnra1arnb, an alternative arrangement with Chariots beside the Guards.

Red's back rank is the first, lowercase rank in FEN; Blue's is the last, uppercase rank. Blue moves first. The General, Cannons, and Soldiers keep the same starting positions in every setup. Nowadays, Janggi is mostly played in North Korea.
